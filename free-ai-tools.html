The AI Model Openness Taxonomy

AF5701 — AI & Data Science in Accounting & Finance  ·  PolyU School of Accounting and Finance

Click a region on the diagram or a legend card below to explore.

Open Models
Architecture, training code, data, and weights all publicly released. Full reproducibility.
OLMo · Pythia · BLOOM · StarCoder2
Open Weights
Weights downloadable, but training data or commercial rights restricted. Often called "open source" — technically not.
Llama 3 · Mistral · DeepSeek · Gemma · Qwen · Phi-4
Free (to Use)
Zero-cost access via web/app. No weights, no control over versioning or data handling.
ChatGPT Free · Gemini Free · Claude Free · Copilot · Grok
Proprietary / Private
Fully closed. Paid API or subscription only. Where most commercial revenue concentrates.
GPT-4o/o3 API · Claude API · Gemini Ultra · Cohere R+
Key Structural Insight: Open Models ⊂ Open Weights — every truly open model has open weights, but the converse is false. The Free circle cuts across both open and proprietary: most open-weight models are free to run locally, while proprietary providers offer free tiers that share no weights. The purely Proprietary region is where governance, audit, and vendor lock-in questions are most acute for finance & accounting.

Using AI Tools Responsibly

Academic Integrity: AI tools are study partners, not substitutes. For all AF5701 deliverables, disclose AI use per PolyU policy. AI-generated text submitted as your own work constitutes academic dishonesty.
Recommended Workflow: (1) Draft your own outline first. (2) Use AI to stress-test, find gaps, suggest alternatives. (3) Revise in your own voice. (4) Cite AI-assisted content in methodology or acknowledgments. This builds genuine competency — what employers and PhD programs value.
